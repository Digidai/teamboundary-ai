# Community code of conduct

TeamBoundary AI welcomes respectful, technically rigorous participation from people of every
background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- give actionable feedback and assume good faith while still protecting security boundaries;
- respect privacy, attribution, licenses and confidential disclosure processes;
- make space for different levels of experience and communicate without harassment; and
- correct mistakes openly when doing so is safe.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, stalking, doxxing, impersonation,
deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or pressuring someone to disclose secrets or
vulnerability details publicly are not acceptable. Repeated bad-faith demands, spam and attempts to
use project channels for abuse are also out of scope.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, project-managed discussions and events, and when someone is
publicly representing the project. It does not give maintainers authority over unrelated private
activity.

## Private reports and enforcement

For a private conduct report, use the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/Digidai/teamboundary-ai/security/advisories/new) and put
`Conduct report` at the start of the title. Do not use a public issue. The project owner reviews the
report, minimizes access to the information and may request additional context.

Proportionate action can include a private warning, content removal, temporary participation limits
or permanent exclusion from project spaces. Immediate protective action may be taken for credible
threats, doxxing or ongoing harassment. A person affected by a decision may request one written
reconsideration through the same private channel.

Because the project currently has one maintainer, independent appeal is not always available. A
conflict involving the maintainer will be documented without exposing the reporter, and an
independent reviewer will be sought before a final non-urgent decision when practical.
