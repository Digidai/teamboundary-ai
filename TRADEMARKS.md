`TeamBoundary AI` is used as an unregistered open-source project identifier. The preliminary screen
in [NAME_CLEARANCE.md](NAME_CLEARANCE.md) does not establish registration, availability,
non-infringement or freedom to operate.

Apache-2.0 licenses copyright and applicable patent rights in the work. It does not grant permission
to use project or contributor trade names, trademarks, service marks or product names except for
reasonable and customary use in describing the work's origin and reproducing NOTICE content.

Forks may truthfully say they are based on TeamBoundary AI and link to the canonical repository.
They must use a distinct name and presentation when necessary to avoid implying that a modified or
hosted version is an official project release, certified service or endorsed partner.

No project logo or certification program is established by this release.

## Rename procedure

If a material naming conflict is identified, the maintainer will:

1. preserve the security and release history under a neutral advisory;
2. choose and screen a replacement name;
3. update source, package, Worker, documentation and repository identifiers;
4. document compatibility aliases without creating a deployable obsolete path; and
5. avoid redirects or announcements that imply ownership of another party's mark.
