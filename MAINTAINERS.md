The roles were accepted through the repository owner's explicit publication instruction. Sensitive
contact details are not stored in the public repository. Security reports use the private route in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Bootstrap limitations

The current bus factor is one. GitHub account recovery and offline repository backups provide
administrative recovery, but they are not independent human review. Material security exceptions,
license changes and commercial claims should wait for an additional qualified reviewer.

New maintainers may be appointed through a recorded governance decision after sustained,
trustworthy contribution. Every added person must explicitly accept the role and disclose material
conflicts of interest.

Code ownership routes review requests; it does not establish copyright ownership or legal
authority. The canonical rules are in [.github/CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS).
