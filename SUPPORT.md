# Support

## Community scope

TeamBoundary AI 0.1.x is an open-source preview with best-effort community support. It is not a
hosted service, service-level agreement, warranty, indemnity or authorized commercial support
offering. The source is provided on the **AS IS** basis described in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Digidai/teamboundary-ai/issues) for reproducible,
non-sensitive source defects and feature proposals. Include the exact version, environment, minimal
synthetic-data reproduction, expected result and actual result. Remove credentials, tenant
identifiers, personal data, prompts, model output and private account details.

Issue acceptance and maintainer discussion are not delivery commitments. Response time and continued
compatibility with every Cloudflare plan, region, limit or model are not guaranteed.

## Security and conduct

Do not report vulnerabilities or private conduct complaints through a public issue. Follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Cloudflare and third-party services

The project is independent of Cloudflare, Inc. Maintainers cannot provide Cloudflare account,
billing, availability, abuse, model or vendor support. Operators must use the applicable vendor
channels and service agreements for third-party infrastructure.

## Hosted and commercial deployments

Apache-2.0 permits commercial use subject to its terms, but a third-party service is not an official
project offering. Every operator must identify its own legal entity, security contact, privacy
terms, support scope, service levels, subprocessors, incident process and billing terms.
